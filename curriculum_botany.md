# JUICY BOTANICAL SCIENCE
## High School & Community Curriculum for Jamaican Flora and Global Botany

### 1. Introduction to Botany
*   **The Lifecycle of a Tree**: From germination to full canopy maturity.
*   **Photosynthesis**: The chemical process of converting sunlight into chemical energy (C6H12O6).
*   **Carbon Sequestration**: How trees like the **Blue Mahoe** (Jamaica's National Tree) act as "carbon sinks" for the environment.

### 2. Biological Science & Environment
*   **Native vs. Invasive Species**: Studying the Jamaican **Lignum Vitae** vs. introduced species.
*   **Growth Rings (Dendrochronology)**: Using wood layers to tell the age of a tree and the history of the climate.
*   **Transpiration**: The movement of water through the plant and its evaporation from leaves.

### 3. Structural Biocomposites
*   **Cellulose & Lignin**: The structural "glue" that gives wood its strength and allows it to be used in construction.
*   **Sustainable Harvesting**: Managing "Cycle" times (e.g., Bamboo's 4yr cycle vs Oak's 100yr cycle).

### 4. Global Comparison
*   **Tropical Hardwoods**: Mahogany, Teak, Cedar.
*   **Temperate Hardwoods**: Oak, Maple, Birch.
*   **Coniferous (Softwoods)**: Pine, Fir, Spruce.
