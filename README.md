## Overview

The JUICY Academy Botanical Science curriculum explores plant biology, ethnobotany, and materials science through Jamaican and Caribbean flora. Jamaica's biodiversity — over 3,000 plant species, 27% endemic — provides one of the richest botanical teaching environments in the world.

Botany here is not separated from culture. Indigenous plant knowledge systems, Maroon herbal traditions, and Rastafarian ital doctrine are treated as legitimate scientific knowledge, not folklore.

## Curriculum Themes

- Plant cell biology and anatomy
- Jamaican endemic species identification
- Ethnobotany: indigenous knowledge as legitimate science
- Photosynthesis and the chlorophyll-CHNOPS connection
- Natural fibers, resins, and composites from Caribbean flora
- Conservation science and endemic species protection
